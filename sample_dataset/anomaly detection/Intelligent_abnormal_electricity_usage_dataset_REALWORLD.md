About Dataset
The dataset was created to support learning and experimentation in electricity usage analysis and abnormal consumption detection.

Access to real-world electricity consumption data is often limited due to privacy and regulatory constraints. To overcome this limitation, this dataset was synthetically generated while being inspired by realistic residential energy usage patterns commonly referenced in smart grid and energy analytics studies.

Dataset Motivation
Real electricity consumption datasets are difficult to share publicly
Privacy and regulatory constraints limit open access
Synthetic data enables safe experimentation without real consumer data

What This Dataset Represents
The dataset simulates residential electricity behavior by combining:

Household characteristics such as dwelling type, number of occupants, and house area
Electrical load and appliance usage patterns
Environmental conditions including temperature and humidity
Expected versus actual energy consumption behavior

Abnormal Usage Concept
Abnormal electricity usage labels are generated based on:

Significant deviation between expected and actual energy consumption
Similarity-based household energy behavior comparison

Intended Use Cases
This dataset is suitable for:

Machine learning classification tasks
Abnormal or anomaly detection research
Exploratory data analysis (EDA)
Academic projects and self-learning

Important Note
All records in this dataset are fully synthetic
No real household, individual, or organization data is included
The dataset is intended strictly for research and educational purposes
